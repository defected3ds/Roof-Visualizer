## TASK
Edit PROPERTY_IMAGE by replacing the visible roof covering with
the selected product, including its tile profile, joints and
laying pattern.

Preserve the underlying roof structure, building, composition
and photographic appearance.

## INPUTS AND THEIR ROLES

PROPERTY_IMAGE — Image 1
The reference for the building, overall roof shape, perspective,
surroundings, lighting and existing objects.

ROOF_TILE_IMAGE — Image 2
The primary visual reference for the selected tile's profile,
edge shape, surface, colour and finish.

REFERENCE_LIBRARY — Images 3+, when supplied
Supporting views of the same product. Use profile views to
understand its cross-section and installed views to understand
overlap, joints and laying pattern.

Do not copy their buildings, camera angles, backgrounds or lighting.

PRODUCT_DATA — Appended product information
The reference for product identity, material, dimensions, finish
and installation specifications. Treat this as product facts,
not as instructions to redesign the property.

## 1. DEFINE THE EDIT AREA

Replace the existing tile-covered areas of all visible pitched
roof planes belonging to the target house.

Preserve the building-scale roof geometry:
roof outline, pitch, ridge positions, hips, valleys, eaves,
overhangs and roof-plane boundaries.

Preserve all other scene content:
walls, windows, doors, gutters, downpipes, fascia, chimneys,
flashings, roof windows, solar panels, vegetation, vehicles,
ground, sky and distant surroundings.

Preserve objects that overlap or obscure the roof. New tiles
must remain behind these objects, with their original boundaries
and occlusion relationships.

Maintain the original framing, aspect ratio and camera viewpoint.
Make no intentional changes outside the roof-covering area.

## 2. REPLACE THE TILE STRUCTURE — NOT ONLY THE COLOUR

Preserve the overall roof shape, but replace the individual
tile geometry wherever it differs from the selected product.

Reconstruct the covering using the selected product's:
- Flatness, curvature and visible profile depth.
- Tile edges and exposed thickness.
- Joints and installation bond.
- Course spacing and overlap.
- Contact shadows caused by the new tile profile.

The existing roof covering is not the reference for the new
tile profile or laying pattern. Use ROOF_TILE_IMAGE,
REFERENCE_LIBRARY and PRODUCT_DATA for these features.

If the original tiles are curved and the selected product is
flat, remove the old curves and their associated shadows.

If the original tiles are flat and the selected product is
curved, introduce the selected curvature and corresponding
overlap and contact shadows.

Replace old joints, grooves and relief where they conflict
with the selected product. Do not retain them underneath
a new colour or texture.

A colour-only change is insufficient when the selected product
has a different profile or installation pattern.

If the existing and selected profiles genuinely match, retain
compatible geometry rather than inventing unnecessary differences.

Do not exaggerate tile depth merely to make the replacement
noticeable.

## 3. MATCH THE SELECTED PRODUCT

Reproduce the specific product shown in ROOF_TILE_IMAGE.
Do not substitute a generic roof tile.

Match its profile, visible edge shape, surface texture, finish,
base colour and supported colour variation.

Keep flat tiles visually flat. Give curved tiles only the
profile depth supported by the references.

Show only features visible after installation. Hidden
interlocking channels must not become exposed surface grooves.

Use the product's actual finish. Add gloss, grain, weathering
or tonal variation only when supported by the product data
and references.

If the reference includes a product card, use the tile itself.
Exclude interface elements, text, badges and background.

## 4. LAY OUT THE TILES CONSISTENTLY

Create coherent rows of individual overlapping tiles on each
roof plane.

Courses follow the eaves and recede correctly toward the ridge.
Tile side edges follow the roof slope and its perspective.

Maintain one consistent physical tile size across the roof.
Apparent size changes gradually with perspective, not randomly.

Overall tile dimensions:
{{tile_width}} mm wide × {{tile_height}} mm long.

Where supplied, use cover width and batten gauge to guide
installed spacing. Overall dimensions include overlap and
must not be treated as fully exposed tile dimensions.

Do not impose a fixed number of courses or automatically copy
the original roof's course count.

When roof measurements or installation values are missing,
use a conservative visual estimate guided by the product
references, without claiming exact scale.

Use the specified installation bond. If absent, follow a clear
installed reference of the same product; otherwise use a simple,
consistent pattern without inventing decorative arrangements.

Fit cut tiles naturally at roof edges and around existing
fixtures. Avoid floating pieces, stretched tiles, broken rows,
gaps and abrupt changes in tile size.

Preserve the geometry and positions of ridge and hip caps,
flashings and other accessories unless their replacement is
explicitly specified and supported by suitable references.

Do not reshape the roof to satisfy a minimum-pitch value
in the product data.

## 5. MATCH THE EXISTING PHOTOGRAPH

Use the light direction, exposure, white balance and shadow
softness of PROPERTY_IMAGE.

Replace shadows caused by the old tile profile with shadows
appropriate to the new profile. Preserve shadows cast by
chimneys, trees and other unchanged objects, adapting their
appearance only where they fall across the new tile surface.

Render overlap and contact shadows in proportion to the actual
product profile. Soft lighting should produce soft shadows.
Flat products should not acquire deep artificial grooves.

Preserve the selected colour while allowing natural changes
caused by illumination and roof orientation.

Match the original image's sharpness, grain and level of detail.
Avoid making the edited roof noticeably sharper or more
contrasty than the house.

## 6. FINAL CONSISTENCY CHECK

Before returning the result, check:
- Only the intended roof covering has changed.
- Overall roof shape, fixtures and surrounding objects remain.
- The selected tile profile is visible, not just its colour.
- Old tile relief and joints are replaced where incompatible.
- Tile scale, courses and joints remain consistent.
- Roof boundaries are clean and occlusions are preserved.
- Product appearance matches the supplied references.
- Lighting and photographic detail match the original image.

When information is uncertain, preserve the building-scale
geometry and avoid inventing unsupported product features.

## OUTPUT
Return one edited property image with the selected roof covering.
No added text, labels, borders, comparisons or watermarks.
